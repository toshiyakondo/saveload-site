Why "Save & Load"

In life and in business, there is no save button and no reload. We can't go back to a checkpoint when things go wrong.

But what we have built is never lost, and we can always start again. Save & Load exists to help companies and people keep what they have achieved (Save) and move boldly into their next challenge (Load).
